1. **Audit before claims.** Recover original sheets, transcribe every interval and note, retain image references and ambiguities. Reconcile written annual totals with interval sums. Until originals arrive, label recovered chat transcriptions honestly; never fabricate earlier curves.
2. **A phone-first almanac.** Warm paper, ink, pumpkin and plum, generous serif headings, concise insights, four stable destinations, readable charts and large tap targets. Full functionality without hover.
3. **Compare the same evening across years.** Persistent year chips; overlay arrivals per 15 minutes, cumulative counts, and actual airport temperature. Touch/keyboard time inspection, explicit gaps, stable year colors, legends and accessible numeric tables.

Keep porch quotations separate; show raw reports, dates, station, method and source links. Do not confuse preceding-hour precipitation with rain at the porch or infer causality from a tiny sample.
5. **A notebook that grows.** Choose or add any year; enter counts in a simple time/count grid, annual total, weather and notes; attach source pictures; review totals and discrepancies before saving. Device-local changes, named backup/restore actions, plain CSV ingestion and preview before import. Preserve the old app's saved records.
6. **Validate the gift.** Data invariants, import rejection/round trips, storage failure behavior; browser interaction tests at 375px and desktop; inspect screenshots, chart gaps, keyboard and touch flows, refresh persistence. Publish a working URL; explicitly distinguish simulated mobile testing from physical iPhone Safari.
